About Olympia Tree Removal Pros

We connect Thurston County homeowners with vetted local tree crews for removal, storm response, stump grinding, and hazard pruning.

How this works

Olympia Tree Removal Pros is a local referral service, not a tree crew. When you call or submit the quote form, your project details go to an established, licensed and insured tree company serving your part of Thurston County. They contact you directly, look at the tree, and give you a quote. You hire them — not us.

We built this site because finding a reliable tree crew in the South Sound is harder than it should be, especially in the days after a windstorm when every contractor's voicemail is full. Our job is to get your request in front of a crew that answers.

What that means for you

No cost to you

Getting matched and receiving an estimate is free. There is no obligation to hire anyone, and we never charge homeowners.

Licensed crews only

We refer to companies that carry Washington contractor registration and liability insurance. Ask any crew for their L&I registration number before work begins — a legitimate contractor will give it to you without hesitation.

You control the decision

We pass along your request. Pricing, scheduling, scope, and the contract are strictly between you and the company you choose. We recommend getting more than one estimate on large removals.

How we are paid

Contractors pay us a referral fee for leads. That fee does not change your price, and we disclose it openly — see our referral disclosure.

Service area

We take requests across Olympia, Lacey, Tumwater, West Olympia, Boston Harbor, and the surrounding Thurston County communities. For an active emergency — a tree on a structure, a trunk across a driveway, or limbs on a power drop — call (360) 822-3284 rather than using the form.

If a tree is contacting a power line, contact Puget Sound Energy and do not approach it.
