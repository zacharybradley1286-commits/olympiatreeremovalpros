How Much Does Stump Grinding Cost in Olympia, WA? (2026 Pricing)

By Olympia Tree Removal Pros

Understand stump grinding pricing in Olympia: diameter rates, root flare calculations, accessibility, and bundle discounts.

Quick Answer · Direct Summary

Stump grinding in Olympia typically costs between $120 and $380 per stump, or $3 to $5 per diameter inch measured across root flares.

Next Question (Is it cheaper to grind multiple stumps at once?): Yes, contractors usually charge a base equipment mobilization fee for the first stump and substantial discounts for each additional stump.

Budgeting for stump grinding cost in Olympia, Washington is straightforward once you understand how tree care contractors measure and price work. Whether you are dealing with a freshly felled conifer or an old weathered stump left by a previous homeowner, knowing the primary cost drivers ensures you receive a fair, transparent quote.

Most professional tree companies in Thurston County calculate stump grinding bids using a per-inch diameter model, typically ranging from $3.00 to $5.50 per inch. Crucially, the measurement is taken across the widest root flare at ground level, not the narrower top surface of the cut log. For a typical twenty-inch Douglas fir stump, grinding costs generally fall between $120 and $220. Larger sixty-inch hardwood stumps with expansive surface roots can range from $300 to $500.

Site accessibility plays a significant role in final pricing. Standard tracked stump grinders weigh upwards of two thousand pounds. If your stump is located in an open front yard, mobilization is fast and efficient. However, if the machine must navigate narrow thirty-six-inch backyard gates, ascend steep retaining wall terraces, or traverse delicate flagstone patios, specialized compact equipment and protective ground mats may incur a modest labor premium.

Homeowners can maximize savings by grouping multiple stumps into a single service visit. Because hauling heavy grinding equipment to your property represents a fixed mobilization cost, contractors often charge a $150 to $200 minimum for the first stump, but as little as $50 to $75 for each subsequent stump. Always ask your contractor about combining stump grinding with hazardous tree trimming or general tree removal for maximum value.

Chip cleanup and haul-away is typically available for an additional modest disposal fee, though many homeowners prefer keeping the mulch for garden beds.
