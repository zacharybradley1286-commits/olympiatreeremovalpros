What Does Tree Removal Cost in Olympia, WA? (2026 Pricing Guide)

By Tree Removal in Olympia Washington Editorial Team

Realistic pricing breakdown for tree removal in Olympia, WA based on height, diameter, crane access, and disposal fees.

Quick Answer · Direct Summary

Residential tree removal in Olympia typically ranges between $450 for small ornamental trees up to $2,800+ for large, hazardous Douglas firs requiring technical rigging.

Next Question (Why do prices vary so drastically between contractors?): Pricing reflects insurance limits, specialized equipment like knuckle-boom cranes, crew sizes, and full haul-away and chipping services.

Understanding tree removal cost in Olympia, Washington helps homeowners budget effectively while distinguishing fair contractor estimates from predatory bids. Because Pacific Northwest timber grows exceptionally tall due to maritime precipitation, regional costs reflect larger trunk diameters and heavier green-wood mass compared to drier parts of the state. Most residential projects fall within predictable pricing tiers based on tree dimensions, site access, and risk factors.

Small trees measuring under thirty feet in height, such as dogwoods, crabapples, and Japanese maples, generally cost between $350 and $650 to remove. Medium trees between thirty and sixty feet, including birch and smaller alder trees, typically average between $700 and $1,400. Large conifers and mature hardwoods exceeding seventy feet—such as historic Douglas firs and multi-stem bigleaf maples—frequently range from $1,500 to $3,200 or more, especially when leaning over structures.

Several distinct cost variables influence your final bid. The proximity to overhead electrical conductors, detached garages, and neighbor boundaries requires slow, manual climbing and technical friction rigging, which increases crew labor hours. Additionally, steep hillside properties along Budd Inlet or ravine corridors off Cooper Point Road limit heavy equipment access, requiring manual haul-away and hand-dismantling.

Homeowners should always review contractor bids to confirm what is included in the bottom-line price. Professional estimates clearly specify wood chipping, log sectioning, haul-away disposal, and site raking. In many instances, pairing tree removal with on-site stump grinding yields significant bundle discounts compared to scheduling separate contractor visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is stump grinding included in tree removal bids?

Usually stump grinding is priced separately, but contractors often offer a 20% to 30% bundle discount when scheduled together.

Does homeowner insurance cover tree removal costs?

Insurance generally covers removal only if a fallen tree has directly damaged an insured structure such as your house or garage.
