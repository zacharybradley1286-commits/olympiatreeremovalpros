Frequently Asked Questions in Boston Harbor

Are shoreline rules different in Boston Harbor?

Yes, and this is the main thing to know. Work within the shoreline jurisdiction near Puget Sound falls under the Shoreline Management Act and Thurston County review in addition to any normal permitting. That review takes real time, so a waterfront removal needs to be planned well ahead — it is not a same-week job unless it is a genuine emergency.

Can a truck actually reach my property?

Sometimes not. Much of the peninsula is served by narrow private drives with tight turns and low limbs, and a chip truck has a real turning radius. We ask about access up front because it changes both the crew and the price, and it is better resolved on the phone than in your driveway.

Why do waterfront trees decline faster?

Constant salt-laden wind off the Sound burns foliage on the exposed side and leaves crowns thin and one-sided. Add winter storms with no windbreak and you get the dieback and top failure common on Boston Harbor conifers. Regular deadwood removal buys years on a tree that would otherwise come out.
