Careers

We are a referral service, so we do not employ climbers, ground crew, or equipment operators directly.

If you run a licensed, insured tree company in Thurston County and want to receive local job requests, get in touch — that is the partnership we do have. Email info@olympiatreeremovalpros.com with your company name, L&I registration number, service area, and the kind of work you take on.

If you are looking for tree work as an individual, contact the established crews in the area directly; we are not able to place you.
