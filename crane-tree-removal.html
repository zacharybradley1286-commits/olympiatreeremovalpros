Why Choose Crane-Assisted Dismantling?

In dense Olympia neighborhoods—like South Capitol, West Olympia, and newer developments in Lacey—mature 100-foot conifers often grow within feet of rooflines, overhead power wires, and delicate septic drain fields. Traditional piecemeal rigging risks property damage if heavy logs swing. Crane removal lifts 2,000 to 5,000-pound picks straight into the sky, eliminating impact.

The Crane Tree Removal Workflow

1. Precision Rigging & Strapping

An arborist attaches high-strength synthetic rigging chokers to the tree section, balancing the load beneath the crane hook before any saw cut is made.

2. Controlled Relief Cut

Under slight crane tension, the climber executes the cut. The crane smoothly lifts the severed section up and away from the canopy without dropping an inch.

3. Street-Side Landing & Chipping

The crane gently deposits the log or branch bundle directly into the landing zone where ground crews process it instantly through the wood chipper.

Frequently Asked Questions: Crane Tree Removal

When is a crane necessary for tree removal in Olympia?

Cranes are deployed when trees are structurally compromised (severe trunk decay, hollow cavities, or root rot) making them unsafe for a climber to ascend, or when there is zero ground drop zone due to roofs, fences, decks, swimming pools, or septic drain fields.

Does crane tree removal damage my driveway or lawn?

No. Professional crane operators utilize heavy composite outrigger pads to distribute machine weight safely across asphalt or concrete driveways. Because tree sections are lifted vertically into the air and swung directly to the street chipper, backyard lawns, fences, and landscaping experience zero impact.

Is crane tree removal more expensive than manual felling?

While mobilization of crane equipment has an hourly cost, crane operations dramatically reduce the crew hours required on-site. Jobs that take two to three days of hazardous climbing and roping can often be completed in a single morning, making crane removal comparable in total price while vastly reducing risk.
