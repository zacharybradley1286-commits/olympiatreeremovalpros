What to do after a storm

  1. Keep people, pets, and vehicles away from unstable trees, hanging limbs, and downed lines.
  2. Do not approach or cut a tree touching utility equipment or a power line.
  3. Document damage from a safe location for your insurer or provider.
  4. Ask the provider to confirm scope, cleanup, and availability directly.

Olympia Tree Removal Pros is a referral service. The independent provider confirms credentials, timing, and contract terms.
