What Affects Tree Removal Cost?

A site visit is the best way to price a tree project. These factors usually matter more than a generic online price range.

Tree size and condition

Height, trunk diameter, decay, lean, and the amount of material to handle can change the work plan.

Access and surroundings

Fences, roofs, driveways, slopes, and utility proximity may affect equipment and rigging needs.

Scope after removal

Confirm whether hauling, chipping, log handling, stump grinding, and site cleanup are included.

Olympia Tree-Project Considerations

Wet soils, mature conifers, and proximity to homes and utilities can make a tree project more complex. A provider should evaluate the site before recommending a work plan.

Within city limits, confirm whether local tree regulations or permit requirements apply to your project.

Questions We Hear

When should I call about a tree hazard?

Call when a tree or limb threatens a structure, driveway, or safe access route. For power-line contact, stay clear and contact the utility first.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Olympia?

Requirements depend on the property and project. Review the City of Olympia resources and confirm permit responsibility with the provider before work starts.

What should a written tree-service estimate include?

Ask for the work scope, access plan, cleanup, debris handling, stump work, permit responsibility, price, and any exclusions.
