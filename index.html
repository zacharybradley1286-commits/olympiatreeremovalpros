Olympia & Thurston County Tree Permit Matrix

Tree removal regulations vary significantly across municipal jurisdictions in the South Sound. Review local permit guidelines before scheduling felling:

Jurisdiction Exemption Rules Permit Required When Key Code Reference
City of Olympia Developed single-family residential lots under 2 acres maintaining minimum tree density. Landmark trees (>24" DBH), critical area buffers, steep slopes, or shoreline zones. OMC Ch. 16.60 & 16.56
City of Tumwater Removal of up to 6 trees per parcel within 3 consecutive calendar years with written waiver. Trees in greenbelts, wetland buffers, historic districts, or exceeding annual allowance. TMC Ch. 16.08
City of Lacey Removal of up to 3 trees within 60 consecutive months under single-family exemption provisions. Removal beyond 3 trees, landmark specimens, or trees within designated open spaces. LMC Ch. 14.32
Unincorporated Thurston County Standard residential maintenance outside critical zones. Properties adjacent to salmon streams, Puget Sound shorelines, or marine bluffs. Thurston Co. CAO (Title 24)

South Sound Tree Species & Soil Hazards

Thurston County's maritime climate creates specific mechanical stresses on native Pacific Northwest trees:

Douglas-fir Root Rot

Pathology: Phellinus weirii (laminated root rot) decays anchor roots beneath mature coastal Douglas-firs.

Risk: Sudden windthrow during November–February squalls without visible crown symptoms.

Bigleaf Maple Codominant Stems

Pathology: Tight V-shaped crotches develop included bark, trapping moisture and rotting the joint.

Risk: Heavy summer leaf weight or winter wet snow causes catastrophic branch failure over roofs.

Glacial Till Saturation

Soil Condition: Dense glacial hardpan prevents drainage, leaving topsoil waterlogged during heavy rains.

Risk: Root plates lift as soil liquifies; visible soil mounding or leaning signals immediate risk.

Network Contractor Standards & Safety

Tree removal is high-risk technical rigging. We connect homeowners exclusively with established independent local contractors who meet stringent operational criteria:

  • Active WA L&I Registration: Verified contractor license in good standing with Washington State Department of Labor & Industries.
  • Commercial Liability Protection: Verified minimum $1,000,000 to $2,000,000 general liability insurance.
  • ANSI Industry Compliance: Pruning performed according to ANSI A300 arboricultural standards and work executed under ANSI Z133 safety codes.
  • Specialized Rigging Capability: Access to friction lowering brakes, tracked spider lifts, and crane-assisted felling for zero lawn impact.
