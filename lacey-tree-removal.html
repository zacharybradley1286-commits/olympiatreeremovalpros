Frequently Asked Questions in Lacey

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Lacey?

It depends on where the tree sits. Lacey regulates removal most tightly near critical areas — wetlands, streams, and the shoreline buffers around Long Lake and Hicks Lake — and on undeveloped or recently platted parcels with canopy retention conditions. A single yard tree on an established residential lot outside those areas is usually far simpler. Confirm with Lacey Community Development before work starts; a crew that tells you no permit is ever needed is guessing.

What makes Hawks Prairie removals different?

Lot lines are close and fences are new. There is often no room to fell a mature fir in one piece, so the tree is dismantled from the top with the pieces roped down. It takes longer than an open-yard drop and it is priced accordingly, but it is the difference between a clean job and a new fence.

A tree came down on my Lacey property overnight — what now?

If it is touching a power line or a service drop, stay away from it and call Puget Sound Energy first. If it is on a structure, photograph it before anything is moved; your insurer will want that. Then call us and we will get a crew routed to you.
