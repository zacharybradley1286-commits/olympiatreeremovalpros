Who You Are Working With

Olympia Tree Removal Pros is a local referral service. We are not the crew that shows up at your house — we are how you get matched to one.

When you call or submit the quote form, your details go to an established tree company that works in your part of Thurston County. They contact you, assess the tree, and quote the job. Your contract is with them.

Because the crew varies by job and area, we do not list a fixed roster here. What we will tell you is what to ask for: the company's Washington L&I contractor registration number and a current certificate of liability insurance. Any legitimate outfit provides both without hesitation.

More on how this works, and how we are paid, on our about page and referral disclosure.
