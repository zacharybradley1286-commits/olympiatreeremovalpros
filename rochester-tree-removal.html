Frequently Asked Questions in Rochester

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Rochester, WA?

Because Rochester is an unincorporated community in Thurston County, tree removals fall under Thurston County regulations rather than a municipal city hall. Standard lot maintenance is generally exempt unless the property is within a designated Critical Area (e.g. Black River floodway, riparian wetlands, or protected Oregon white oak woodlands under Title 24).

Why do windstorms cause heavy tree damage in Rochester?

Rochester and Grand Mound sit along the Chehalis River gap, which channels intense Pacific storm winds directly inland. Combined with sandy alluvial soils and a high winter water table, shallow-rooted Douglas-firs and mature alders are particularly prone to root plate failure.

Can you handle stump grinding on rural pasture lots?

Yes. Network contractors deploy high-horsepower tracked stump grinders that can access acreage fields, pastures, and wooded fence lines, grinding stumps and surface roots well below mower depth.
