Tree Services in Olympia, WA

Four core services covering the full life of a problem tree — from the assessment that decides whether it needs to come out at all, through removal, cleanup, and the stump.

Which service do you need?

Most people call us about one of four situations. If you are not sure which fits, describe what you are seeing when you call and we will sort it out.

Tree Removal

For trees that are dead, structurally failing, or too close to a structure to keep. On tight Olympia lots this means sectional dismantling rather than felling — the tree comes down in pieces roped to the ground.

Choose this if: the tree is dead, has a recent lean with soil heaving, has significant trunk decay, or simply has to go for a project.

Stump Grinding

Grinding the remaining stump and root flare 6 to 12 inches below grade so the area can be reseeded. Cheaper scheduled alongside a removal than as a separate return trip.

Deadwooding, crown reduction, and clearance pruning — the preventative work that keeps a tree you want to keep. Often the right answer when removal is not.

When removal is not the answer

Not every problem tree needs to come out, and any crew that recommends removal for every tree they look at is worth a second opinion. A healthy tree with a long-standing lean, deadwood in the crown, or limbs over a roof can usually be managed with pruning or crown reduction at a fraction of the cost of removal.

The situations that genuinely call for removal are structural: substantial decay in the trunk or root collar, a recent lean with cracked or heaving soil on the uphill side, major root damage from excavation, or a dead tree within striking distance of something that matters. If you are unsure which describes your tree, that is what the assessment is for.
