Frequently Asked Questions in Yelm

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Yelm?

Within Yelm city limits, single-family residential properties outside critical areas generally do not require a separate tree removal permit for routine hazard mitigation, but land clearing and commercial site changes fall under YMC Title 18. Properties in unincorporated Thurston County surrounding Yelm are subject to the Thurston County Critical Areas Ordinance if near the Nisqually River, wetlands, or salmon streams.

How does acreage tree removal differ from urban residential work in Yelm?

Acreage properties in Yelm and Rainier often have space for traditional directional felling using wedges and pull lines rather than slow sectional dismantling. This allows faster clearing of multiple hazardous Douglas-firs or pasture boundary trees at a lower cost per tree.

What should I do if a tree falls across a fence or driveway in Yelm?

If power lines or utility poles are affected, immediately call Puget Sound Energy. If lines are clear, document the damage for insurance adjusters, then call our dispatch line at (360) 822-3284 to route an emergency local crew for clearing and bucking.
